netko/brand/essence

A cat with a terminal

Netko is an open-source lab. We hack on LLMs for fun and ship the cool tools that come out of it, including desktop frameworks, SQL clients, self-hosting platforms and chat playgrounds. The brand should feel like the place those experiments happen: a terminal at 2am with a cat on the desk.

Curious

We poke at models to see what they really do, then build the tool we wished existed.

Playful

Our cat walks across the keyboard sometimes. We call those features. Fun is the reason we start.

Precise

Fun is no excuse for flaky. Typed end to end, documented, and shipped one commit at a time.

The mark, in one breath

The cat is the conversation: a chat bubble with ears, drawn the way a terminal would draw it. Square corners, a tail that steps down in pixels, a > for the eye that’s typing and a pink square for the eye that’s watching.

netko/brand/logo/mark

The mark

One mark, two colors. Sumi or kami for the body, sakura for the eye. The pink eye is the only part that ever changes: it blinks, it reacts, and it stays pink.

netko/brand/logo/lockups

Lockups

The wordmark is drawn on a 5 × 7 pixel grid, not typed. Its stroke matches the face strokes, so mark and word read as one object. Always write it lowercase: netko.

netko/brand/logo/construction

Construction

Everything sits on a 256-unit grid in 16-unit steps. When the mark needs adjusting, adjust it here first. Never redraw it by eye.

  • Body: 208 × 140 on a 256 grid, corner radius 14. Corners stay nearly square, never pill-shaped.
  • Ears: apex 48 units above the body, with only 6 units of rounding. Sharp enough to read at 16px.
  • Eye: a 30-unit square ring with a 14-unit stroke. That stroke is the brand’s “pixel”.
  • Tail: two 18-unit steps, left-aligned at x = 56. It steps, it never curves.
  • Face: 18-unit strokes with square caps and miter joins. No rounded ends anywhere in the mark.

netko/brand/logo/space-and-size

Clear space & minimum size

Clear space equals one eye (30 units, about ⅛ of the mark) on every side. Nothing else enters that box, not even other logos.

pink square = one eye = clear space

Minimum sizes

16px

32px

48px

lockup 120px

  • At 32px and below, use the pixel cut (see pixel cut). It’s hand-placed on a 16 × 16 grid.
  • From 48px up, use the vector mark.
  • The horizontal lockup never goes narrower than 120px. Below that, use the mark on its own.

Don’ts

don’t rotate or tilt
don’t stretch
don’t recolor the eye
no shadows, glows or bevels

netko/brand/character/expressions

Expressions

The mark is also the mascot. Swapping the face turns it into UI: toasts, empty states, CLI output, error pages. The pink eye always carries the emotion.

Idle >.□

Default. Logo, avatars, anything not reacting.

Happy ^.^

Success, a merged PR, a new release.

Loading ...

Waiting on a model or a build. The pink token steps through.

Error x.x

Something broke. Always next to text that says what broke and how to fix it.

Sleep -.-

Empty states, offline, nothing here yet.

  • Only one expression on screen at a time. The cat has one mood.
  • Never invent a face outside this set without adding it here first.
  • Error always comes with plain text that says what broke and how to fix it.

netko/brand/character/pixel-cut

Pixel cut

Below 48px, vector curves turn to mush. The pixel cut is the same cat placed by hand on a 16 × 16 grid. Scale it only in whole multiples (16, 32, 64) so every pixel stays crisp.

netko/brand/system/type

Type

Two faces: a pixel mono that talks like a terminal, and a rounded sans that keeps the long reads friendly. Both are free and open-license (SIL OFL) and ship in the kit.

display · labels · code

Departure
Mono >_

Headlines, UI labels, numbers, code, CLI output. Use it sparingly at large sizes, where it gets loud.

body · long reads

M PLUS
Rounded 1c

Paragraphs, docs and descriptions. Its soft terminals are the kawaii half of the brand. Regular for body text, Bold for emphasis.

netko/brand/system/voice

Voice

Curious, playful, precise. We sound like a friend explaining a hack they’re proud of: specific, a little funny, never selling.

Talk like a README, not a press release.

say

Mirin builds desktop apps with Bun and Chromium.

don’t say

Mirin revolutionizes the future of desktop development.

Say what broke and how to fix it.

say

Port 5432 is taken. Stop the other Postgres or set DB_PORT.

don’t say

Oops! Something went wrong 😿

One joke per page. The cat is a seasoning.

say

Shipped one commit at a time, unless the cat hits enter early.

don’t say

Purr-fectly paw-some tools for your meow-chine!

Be honest about LLMs.

say

Drafts the SQL with an LLM. You review it before anything runs.

don’t say

AI-powered magic that just works.
